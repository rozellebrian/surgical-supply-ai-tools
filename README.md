Surgical Supply AI Tools

A portfolio of Claude-native AI tools and automated workflows built to solve real operational problems in a 15-room OR surgical supply department — implant tracking, recall monitoring, coding, and supply chain automation.

Tools
HCPCS C-Code Lookup (OR-specific)

Looks up HCPCS C-codes for OR implants and devices.

HCPCS Lookup (hospital-wide)

General-purpose HCPCS Level II lookup covering all series (A, B, C, E, G, J, K, L, M, Q, R, S, T, V) across hospital departments, not just the OR.

Implant Name Formatter

Looks up a surgical implant by reference/catalog number and returns a standardized name in OR naming convention: [Item Type] [Modifiers] [Size/Spec] | [Vendor] | [Catalog #] | [HCPCS Code]. Includes a toggle for code-only vs. full format output.

Surgical Recall Tracker

Pulls and summarizes FDA medical device recalls daily against a 26-vendor watch list (Arthrex, Medline, Medtronic, Stryker, Acumed, Alphatec, Spinal Elements, Zimmer Biomet, Precision Spine, and others).

Supply Substitute Finder

Finds substitute/equivalent products for general OR and hospital supplies (gloves, drapes, sutures, catheters, etc.) — excludes implants, hardware, and biologics.

OR Supply Ordering App

Streamlines ordering and tracking of OR supplies.

Expired Supply Waste Tracker

Mobile-first SharePoint List + Power Apps canvas app for tracking expired medical supply waste. Two-phase capture/pricing workflow with multi-user access and a grouped monthly view for reporting. In production.

Value Analysis (VA) Submission Tracker

SharePoint List with two Power Automate flows automating the value analysis submission and review process:

Flow 1: On new submission, emails the business director with Description, Surgeon, Additional Info, and submitter (Created By).
Flow 2: On director decision (Approved/Denied), emails the original coordinator with Status, Comments, and Trial Info.
Background

Built by Brian Rozelle, Lead Surgical Supply Coordinator at AHC Shady Grove Medical Center, to reduce manual coordination overhead across implant tracking, recall management, and supply chain workflows spanning Cerner/Discern, Workday, and UDI systems.
